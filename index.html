Questions

Quick answers before you start.

Is it free?

Yes. DualChat is free to use for now.

Which links do I paste?

For YouTube, the link of your live stream (it looks like youtube.com/watch?v=…). For Kick, your channel name or link.

Why is my YouTube chat blank?

YouTube only shows chat for a stream that is live or scheduled, with chat turned on. Check that and try again.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. On a phone the two chats stack, with buttons at the bottom to switch between them.
